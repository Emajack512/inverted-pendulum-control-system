# Bibliography

Main references used for the modeling, analysis, simulation and control design of the inverted pendulum system.

## Books

* Ogata, K. *Modern Control Engineering*. 5th Edition. Pearson.
* Kuo, B. C. *Automatic Control Systems*. Prentice Hall.

## Course Material

* Course notes from *Sistemas de Control* UNSL FICA.
* Lecture slides on mathematical modeling, time-domain analysis, root locus, frequency response and state-space control.
* *Sistemas de Control* course program, Universidad Nacional de San Luis.

## Related Academic Work
